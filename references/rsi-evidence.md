# RSI mechanisms relevant to choosing experiments

Current source check: 2026-09-28. These are **published task results**, never proof of an NSI/FRM PSNR gain. Recheck latest primary versions before using a mechanism as a research reference. Prefer the smallest local ablation that tests the transfer claim.

| Decision | Mechanism and measured evidence | Transfer test / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Trial first | [FML-Bench v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17373v2), 18 research-code tasks, equal 100-step budget: switch from greedy to several branches after validation stagnation; mean normalized **test** improvement 0.208 vs greedy AutoResearch 0.192 and TAS v2 0.193. | Under the same FRM compute and evaluation budget, compare predeclared greedy search with predeclared stagnation-triggered branching. Do not spend extra runs or select the trigger on the final test. The reported margin is modest and does not establish FRM transfer. |
| Keep as evaluation design | [AIDE² v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.26457v1), 99 rewrite proposals in an 8-day run, 7 accepted; its selection-benchmark private grade rose 0.703→0.778, with checks on four external benchmarks. Its inner research agent optimizes a visible signal while outer selection uses held-out outcomes at fixed budget. | Separate the agent-visible development score from confirmation; compare research policies at equal total cost. The 0.703→0.778 lineage is selection on that private grade, so external benchmarks matter. Its 3-seed outer-loop comparison with a human-engineered agent ended 0.780 vs 0.782 and was inconclusive; do not claim self-improvement beats human direction. |
| Trial only after plateau | [Darwin Gödel Machine v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22954v3) uses an archive of past agent versions rather than always extending the current winner. Its parent-selection ablation reports SWE-bench 50.0% vs greedy 39.7%; Polyglot subset 38.0% vs 30.0%. | Coding-agent benchmark, not image restoration. A small archive after stagnation is a candidate; a full open-ended agent-evolution system is not justified by these FRM data. Preserve total compute and compare against simple branching. |
| Human-guidance trial, not established FRM gain | [RExBench v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.22598v3), executable research extensions: in its newer-model appendix, static expert location hints raised Claude 4.5 Opus success from 42% to 62%, while step-by-step hints reached 50%. [AutoResearchClaw v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20025) compares scripted expert intervention schedules, with CoPilot 87.5% vs full-auto 25% LLM-judged paper acceptance on a small set. | Neither is a controlled live-human FRM metric study. Ask the researcher only for high-value information: scientific objective, suspected wrong premise, protected mechanism, or where to inspect; let the model design and execute discriminating tests. Locally compare no hint, concise location/correction hint, and detailed prescription under the same budget. |
| Evidence-integrity gate, not a score booster | [Implementation Lottery](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26587v1) audits 312 idea-to-artifact assignments: one-realization winners reverse under independent implementations in 25.6%/43.6% of sampled decisions, with setting-dependent uncertainty. | If choosing an idea from a narrow gain, repeat the idea-to-code translation independently as well as runs of the saved artifact. The paper does not show that this gate itself improves best attainable FRM PSNR. |
| Conditional statistical gate | [Statistical Gödel Machines v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.10232v2) separates exploration from fresh confirmation and reduces false acceptance in simulations, but at one reported setting final utility is 1.25385 vs 1.30272 for per-test acceptance, using more observations. | Use when a false scientific claim is costlier than missed weak gains; do not call it a task-score improvement mechanism. The paper's ImageNet-100 adoption is small and does not certify the older exploratory trajectory. |
| Evaluator pattern only | [AlphaEvolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13131) gives human-defined evaluator and edit scope with programmatic checking. The [official result repository](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphaevolve_results) verifies selected mathematical discoveries, not the full agent search. | Borrow the explicit evaluator and protected-code boundary. Do not assert that its full loop is independently reproduced, or that human task-setting has a quantified causal effect. |

**Admission rule:** An intervention becomes a confirmed **task-gain** rule only after a fair primary-metric improvement and untouched confirmation at a matched budget. A mechanism claim can instead be supported by a reproducible discriminating intervention, even when the model does not beat the baseline; do not call that a performance gain. Exploratory signals can justify the next experiment but cannot be promoted to confirmation. A search-policy improvement needs comparison of **research trajectories at equal total cost**, not only the best model from the more expensive trajectory. Keep each rule scoped to its measured task and retain a falsifier.
